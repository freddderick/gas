## The short version

- No account, no sign-in, no email address.
- Your location is used only to fetch the pressure forecast for that place.
- Pressure data and your log are stored only to provide alerts and exports.
- Your data is never sold.

## What the app uses, and why

**Location.** When you choose "Use my current location", the app reads your location once. If you search for a city, it uses that city's location instead. The app sends the location to our alert server, which rounds it to an area about 10 km across before storing it. The exact position is never stored on the server. The location is used only to fetch the pressure forecast for that area. The app does not track your location in the background.

**Anonymous install ID and push token.** On first launch the app creates a random install ID and secret, which are not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. If you allow notifications, Apple gives the app a push token. Both are stored on our server, only so it can send you alerts.

**Alert settings and alert history.** Your thresholds, quiet hours, unit and time zone are stored on our server so it can decide when to alert you. It also keeps a list of the alerts it sent, so you can see them in the app.

**Pressure data.** The server stores the hourly sea-level pressure for each rounded area, from Open-Meteo, for up to 14 days.

**Headache and pain log.** Your log entries stay on your device. They are never sent to our server. They leave your device only if you export them yourself.

**Phone barometer.** If you open the phone barometer screen, the app reads your phone's pressure sensor while that screen is open. The reading is not stored or sent anywhere.

## What we don't do

No advertising. No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your data.

## Third parties

- **Open-Meteo** (open-meteo.com) provides the forecast. Our server, not your phone, sends Open-Meteo the rounded coordinates of each area. No information about you is sent.
- **Apple Push Notification service** delivers alerts to your device.
- Our server is hosted by [hosting provider] in [country].

## Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data from the alert server. This removes your install ID, push token, settings and alert history from the server straight away. Deleting the app removes everything on your device. Server records for installs that stop checking in are deleted automatically after 90 days without the app being opened.
